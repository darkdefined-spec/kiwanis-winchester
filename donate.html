Every Dollar Stays Local

The Kiwanis Club of Winchester is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law and go directly toward programs serving Winchester children, families, and community organizations.

Where Your Gift Goes

Youth Scholarships

Annual scholarships awarded to outstanding Key Club members who've demonstrated a commitment to service and leadership.

K Clubs & Youth Programs

Supporting Key Clubs at 3 high schools and Builder's Clubs at 3 middle schools across the Winchester area.

Community Grants

Financial support distributed to local organizations and causes serving children, families, and the Winchester community — including 20% of Pancake Day proceeds to ChildSafe Center.

Community Events & Service

Funding the club's annual events — Pancake Day, Kids Christmas Party, Salvation Army bell ringing, highway cleanup, and blood drives.

Tax Deductibility

The Kiwanis Club of Winchester is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the full extent permitted by law. A receipt will be provided for your records.
