Community Support

Requesting Financial Support or a Grant

Winchester Kiwanis raises and distributes funds for projects that benefit children, youth leadership, families, and the local community. Organizations seeking support should email the club secretary with a short, specific request.

Email sec@winvakiw.org

Include this in your request

Your organization name and primary contact

The program, project, or need you're asking Kiwanis to support

The requested amount or type of support

Timeline, deadlines, and any event dates

How it benefits children, students, families, or area residents
