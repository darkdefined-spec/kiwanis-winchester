Become a Member

Have you been looking to volunteer for good causes? Do you like helping kids and feeling like you made a difference? There's a place for you here.

Why Kiwanis?

More Than Volunteering — It's a Community

Kiwanis membership is about friendship, purpose, and impact. Here's what you gain when you join the Winchester club.

Make a Real Impact

Every program we run directly benefits children and families in Winchester. Your time and effort create lasting change in your own backyard.

Build Real Friendships

Weekly meetings and events create the kind of friendships that last for years. Kiwanis members often describe the club as a second family.

Develop as a Leader

Kiwanis offers leadership opportunities at every level — from running a single event to holding elected club office and participating in district programs.

Join a Global Network

You'll be part of Kiwanis International — a network of volunteers in 80+ countries, all united by the same commitment to improving the world for children.

Kids in Winchester need people who show up.

Every Wednesday at noon, Kiwanis members do exactly that — for over a century.

Simple Process

How to Get Started

Joining Kiwanis is straightforward. No complicated application — just reach out and we'll take it from there.

1

Send Us an Email

Email our club president at pres@winvakiw.org to express your interest. A member will reach out to answer any questions and help get you started.

pres@winvakiw.org
2

Visit a Meeting

Come as a guest to one of our Wednesday noon meetings at the Winchester Moose Club (215 E. Cork St.). No commitment required — just show up and see what we're about.

3

Join the Club

When you're ready, formally join as a Kiwanis member. You'll gain full access to all programs, events, and the broader Kiwanis International community.

Questions

Common Questions

Do I need any special skills or background to join?

No. Kiwanis welcomes anyone who wants to serve their community. Whether you have decades of experience or have never volunteered before, there's a role for you.

How much time do I need to commit?

Weekly Wednesday noon meetings are about an hour. Service projects vary — some are a few hours, others are longer events like Pancake Day. You participate at whatever level works for your schedule.

Can I attend a meeting before committing?

Absolutely — guests are always welcome. Come have lunch with us on any Wednesday at noon. No RSVP needed, no pressure to join.

Is there a membership fee?

Yes, there are annual dues. Contact us at pres@winvakiw.org for current membership fee information.
